# Quick Start Guide

Get started with DocuLock in 5 minutes. This guide will walk you through setting up your wallet and verifying your first document on the Sui blockchain.

<Callout type="info">
  DocuLock runs on the Sui Testnet. You'll need SUI test tokens which can be obtained from the [Sui Faucet](https://faucet.testnet.sui.io/).
</Callout>

## Prerequisites [#prerequisites]

Before you begin, ensure you have:

- A Sui-compatible wallet (Sui Wallet, Suiet, or other supported wallets)
- SUI testnet tokens
- A document to verify (PDF, image, text file, etc.)

## Step 1: Connect Your Wallet [#step-1-connect-wallet]

1. Open the DocuLock application
2. Click the **"Connect Wallet"** button in the top-right corner
3. Select your preferred wallet from the list
4. Approve the connection in your wallet extension

<Callout type="warning">
  Make sure your wallet is set to the **Sui Testnet**. DocuLock currently operates on testnet for testing purposes.
</Callout>

## Step 2: Create a Document Registry [#step-2-create-registry]

Before uploading documents, you need to create a document registry:

1. Navigate to the **Upload** tab
2. If you don't have a registry, you'll see a "Create Registry" option
3. Click **"Create Registry"**
4. Sign the transaction in your wallet
5. Wait for the transaction to confirm

<Callout type="success">
  You only need to create a registry once! All your documents will be stored in this registry.
</Callout>

## Step 3: Upload Your Document [#step-3-upload-document]

1. Click the **"Select File"** button or drag and drop your document
2. Supported file types include:
   - PDF documents
   - Images (PNG, JPG, GIF, etc.)
   - Text files
   - Office documents
3. DocuLock will automatically calculate the SHA-256 hash of your document
4. Review the hash displayed on screen

## Step 4: Store on Blockchain [#step-4-store-blockchain]

1. Click **"Store on Blockchain"**
2. Review the transaction details in your wallet
3. Sign and approve the transaction
4. Wait for confirmation (typically 2-5 seconds)
5. Your document hash is now permanently stored on Sui!

## Step 5: Verify Your Document [#step-5-verify-document]

After storing, you can verify your document anytime:

### Method 1: Using the Verify Tab

1. Navigate to the **Verify** tab
2. Select the same document
3. DocuLock will calculate the hash and check against the blockchain
4. If the hash matches, your document is verified as authentic!

### Method 2: Using QR Code

1. After successful storage, click **"Generate QR"**
2. Scan the QR code with your mobile device
3. The verification link will open and verify the document automatically

## Troubleshooting [#troubleshooting]

### Transaction Failed

If your transaction fails:

- Ensure you have sufficient SUI tokens in your wallet
- Check that you're connected to Sui Testnet
- Verify your network connection

### Hash Not Found

If verification shows "hash not found":

- Confirm the document hasn't been modified
- Check that you're querying the correct network (testnet)
- Ensure the original transaction was successful

## Security Tips [#security-tips]

- Your document content never leaves your device
- Only the cryptographic hash is stored on blockchain
- Keep your private keys secure
- Always verify documents after uploading

<Callout type="info">
  Remember: DocuLock only stores hashes, not file contents. Your privacy is protected by design.
</Callout>
